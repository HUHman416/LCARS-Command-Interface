# Version 31.5.12 Development — TOS Console Controls

This applies the comparative audit of the supplied television-prop and Bridge Crew Original Series references. The small square/dome experiment was an intermediate local preview. The operator then authorized the audit's recommended changes.

Large square resin caps now dominate. Sidebar station caps are 48 pixels on tall desktops and 40 pixels at ordinary desktop height; deck action caps are 40 pixels, with 36-pixel caps in compact layouts. Selector caps are 34 pixels on desktop and 30 pixels in compact layouts. Printed legends sit beside the sockets. A small number of explicitly chosen actions use seated domes; ordinary action keys and auxiliary controls remain square. Tinted edges, darker cores, localized reflections, ivory/amber/blue/ruby colors, readable pale legends, and black sockets follow the references without adding green dominance.

Inset black mounting plates group display selectors, bank selectors, active controls, and header actions within the existing connected gray housing. The sidebar body remains one continuous gray casting. Legacy flat blue backgrounds behind caps are removed. Controls share the available space within each page, including banks with fewer keys; narrower decks use three-control pages so larger caps do not squeeze the captions. Telemetry controls now have a separate engineering bank. Search and Calendar have compact record headings; Calendar's selected-date strip is black and its date keys are compact squares.

The information glass stays black, without blue trim. Named information sheets and fixed control banks remain. Below 700 pixels high the command deck starts stowed behind COMMAND KEYS, allowing the screen to use the available height; reopening it prioritizes access to the hardware on very short displays. All original controls stay mounted, preserving native forms, values, callbacks, keyboard feedback, disabled states, focus, and accessible names. Horizontal captain's-chair rockers keep their simple painted faces and explicit ON/OFF states. Reflections remain static. Other console families keep their original control markup and styling. Full bridge ambiance, startup audio, all earlier cues, and local fonts remain.

Validation: desktop and hosted builds; 224 JavaScript tests and 69 Python tests; syntax and whitespace checks. The seven previously recorded TypeScript diagnostics remain. Browser inspection covered all eleven sections at 1280×720: 55 logical banks and 70 key pages, plus 33 information sheets in the audited bank states, with no overflowing hardware captions or clipped graphics. Portrait 390×844 and short landscape 800×420 were checked, including command-bank stowing, larger sparse banks, Search, Calendar, horizontal rockers, high contrast, and switching to Enterprise-D and back. Sheet totals adapt to available height and the active control bank. Native Windows/Android device rendering and audio playback were not locally verified.

The supplied reference images remain outside the public repository. No new audio asset is introduced in this release. Version 30 remains Stable; 31.6 stays reserved for Security, Recovery, and Accessibility.
